# 30-day organic distribution calendar

Day 1 is the first authorized publication day; no campaign has started. Owner: one operator, asynchronous work only. Week budgets: 5h, 4h, 4h, 4h, plus 1h on days 29–30 (18h total). Budgets include written replies and measurement. If permission or output verification is pending, complete local preparation and shift publication dates; do not compress into a posting blast.

Tracking codes: F = Figma listing UTMs; C/S/P = client/SaaS/portfolio guide source redirect; V = video-description UTMs; W = community-post UTMs; R = read-only weekly scorecard. Links are in publication-copy.md and walkthrough-scripts.md. UTMs require an analytics reader; outbound clicks alone are not installations. No paid advertising.

## Weekly decision gates

Week 1 (5h): verify free export remains available; checkout terms agree with configured $4 one-time / $5 monthly price. All new links work before posting. A live payment is not required to publish a written tutorial, but do not claim a tested complete purchase journey without test-mode confirmation.

Week 2 (4h): aim for at least 20 qualified visits per tutorial before comparing channels. Below that, keep counts and continue useful distribution; no conversion “winner.” If visitors click install but activation cannot be joined, report the gap.

Week 3 (4h): target 12/30 matured upgrade actors reaching checkout (40%) as an internal decision threshold, not a forecast. Below that, review email/error/offer states before expanding reach. Stop and investigate if an identified checkout error blocks purchase or free export success falls materially with comparable versions/cohorts.

Week 4 (4h): prioritize the channel with attributable first exports and buyers per hour of work, within available attribution. Recurring revenue test: look for new subscribers who export on a later week and renew when due. No subscription claim from Pass purchases or trial access. If repeat exporting remains 1–2 actors, focus on reusing master examples and useful return visits; do not expand features.

Days 29–30 (1h): preserve channels with useful usage signals and manageable written-support load. If no new buyers and fewer than 30 matured upgrade actors, call the result inconclusive. If ≥30 and no paid conversions, revisit the purchase journey before producing more variants. Suggested support cap is two hours/week; exceeding it shifts content time toward fixing the repeated confusion, not hiring a sales/onboarding operation.
