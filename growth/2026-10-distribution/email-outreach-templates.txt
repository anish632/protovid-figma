3) REPLY TEMPLATE — "How do you share prototypes with clients?"
   (Reddit r/FigmaDesign, r/UI_Design, Twitter/X threads)
============================================================
Read the thread first. Only post if a video genuinely answers their question.
Lead with the advice, mention the tool once, never spam the link in multiple
replies.

---
Sharing the raw Figma link usually backfires — clients click the wrong frame,
get lost, and you end up on a call walking them through navigation.

What's worked for me: send a video instead. They just press play. A screen
recording works in a pinch, but it picks up your cursor and notifications and
compresses badly.

I use a plugin called ProtoVid that exports the connected frames straight to an
MP4/GIF (no recording) and gives you a review link where the client can leave
timestamped comments without a Figma account. Free tier is enough to try it.
